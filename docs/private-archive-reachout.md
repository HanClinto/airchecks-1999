# Private aircheck archives — reach-out plan (target ~April 27 – May 11, 1999)

## Why this exists

The archive.org `airchecks` collection does not contain a play of Will Smith's
"Wild Wild West" (verified by search: no `text:"wild wild west"` 1999 hits;
no title/subject hits; no `creator:"smith"` 1999 hits). The catalog's flagship
pursuit is the hype around that release — early partner-station and private
station airchecks from the week before/around the May 4–11, 1999 general release.

## Release anchor

- "Wild Wild West" — Will Smith, lead single from *Willennium*, released
  ~May 4 – 11, 1999 (sources differ on the exact date; treat May 4 as the
  earliest target and May 11 as the latest).
- Target window: roughly **April 27 – May 11, 1999**.

## Acquisition plan (gated on your explicit approval of each send)

1. **Verify the collection contents** — ask each collector, via their public
   email, what's in their 1999/April-May 1999-era holdings and whether any
   segment of Wild Wild West is present.
2. **State intent clearly** — catalog annotation + crossposting + archive
   expansion only; no re-hosting; no distribution; audio kept local and
   private; delivered specimens are survey-only (fingerprints + match results).
3. **Do NOT pay out unless** a confirmed segment of Wild Wild West is in
   the collection and a small-fee / trade is acceptable. Archival records
   for collectors should not require licensing a radio recording of a
   copyrighted song beyond fair-use cataloging. Defaulting to **trade/swap**
   (audio for documented catalog entries) over purchase.
4. **Match protocol** — if a segment is acquired, use `fpcalc` to fingerprint
   it, build the match result into the catalog, and compare. The audio stays
   local; the catalog records fingerprint + match + in-catalog usage metadata
   only.
